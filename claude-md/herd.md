## Multi-agent roles (herd)

`HERD_ROLE` set means you run in my herdr multi-agent setup; your role skill and `~/.claude/herd/PROTOCOL.md` govern you. Only the `coordinator` talks to me and uses `decide`. A `manager` escalates questions as a decide-format file in its herd outbox; `worker` and `reviewer` put questions in their report for the manager.
